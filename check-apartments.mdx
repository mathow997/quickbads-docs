## Check Apartments

1. Open a floor plan view.
2. Go to the **QuickBADS** tab and click **Check Apartments**.
3. A report opens. It lists every apartment found on the level, what was checked and the result. Click **Close**.

The fills, labels, circulation paths and apartment tags are now on the plan.

QuickBADS finds apartments from your walls, doors and furniture. You don't need to place Revit rooms, and any rooms you have
are not used for grading.

## Live Update

With **Live Update: On**, QuickBADS rechecks the level after every change, such as moving a wall, a bed or a table. The update
waits until nothing is selected, so fills don't jump while you are still editing. Click the button to turn it off. You can still
check by hand.

<Tip>
  Live update rechecks the whole level. On a large floor plate each update takes a few seconds. Turn Live Update off while you
  make big changes, then click **Check Apartments**.
</Tip>

## Clear Fills

**Clear Fills** removes everything QuickBADS drew in this view. It also drops any edits you made to fills, paths and storage
(see [Edit the fills](/editing)). Apartment names and schedule values are stored on the entry doors, so they stay.

## The report

Each check writes the report to `last-report.txt` in the add-in folder, so you can keep it with your project records.

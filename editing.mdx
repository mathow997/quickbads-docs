---
title: "Edit the fills"
description: "Move, resize and reshape fills, paths and storage. Your edits stay when the plan is rechecked."
---

QuickBADS places every fill for you, but you can override it. Your edits are kept on every recheck and graded at their new size.

## Room boxes

- **Move or resize** a bedroom, living or bathroom box with its grips. It keeps your size, moves with the walls it sits against,
  and regrades.
- A box against furniture follows the furniture. If you drag a box over a table or chairs, it snaps back to them.
- **Swap the type** in the Properties type selector (for example Bath A → Bath B). The box takes the new type's default size.

## Circulation paths

Reshape or delete a path like any filled region. It stays that way when the plan is rechecked. To put it back, select the path (or
the apartment's entry door) and click **Reset Path**.

## Storage fills

- Move, reshape or delete a storage fill. It counts at its new area × its type's height. A deleted fill counts as 0 m³.
- Change its type (full height / under-bench / overhead) in Properties.
- Draw your own storage fill to test whether more storage would fit.

To put a storage fill back, select it and click **Reset Storage**.

<Warning>
  **Clear Fills** resets all edits in the view.
</Warning>
